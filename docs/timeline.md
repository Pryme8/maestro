# Timeline

The Timeline page shows the machine over time: load, what happened, what agents spent, and achievements. All of it is kept in files on this computer and sent nowhere.

## Timeline

The last hour, 6 hours or 24 hours, one line a minute: processor load, memory, download and upload, with a mark wherever something happened. Hover for that minute's figures.

Maestro keeps recording while its window is minimised. It records nothing while it is not running, and those stretches show as gaps. One day of minutes is kept.

## Events

Every action Maestro took for you, every warning, each agent starting or stopping, and each achievement, newest first. The last 400 are kept.

## Ledger

What each agent reported spending today, over 7 days, over 30 days and in total, with a row for all agents together. The figures come from [usage reports](agents.md#usage-reports), so an agent that does not report shows nothing. Up to 400 days are kept.

This is not the same as the accounts ledger on the [Router](router.md) page, which lists your AI accounts and their allowances.

## Log book

Thirteen achievements, such as keeping the processor calm for an hour or finishing ten quests, each with its progress. They are judged from running counts that only go up, and none of them rewards spending more.
